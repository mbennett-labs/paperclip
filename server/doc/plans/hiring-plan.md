## 1. Software Engineer

### Summary
Develop and maintain email-related features, ensuring system reliability.

### Expertise & Responsibilities
- Implement and test email communication features.
- Collaborate with the product team to refine requirements.
- Ensure system reliability and performance.

### Priorities
1. Feature development.
2. System reliability.
3. Collaboration with QA and DevOps.

### Boundaries
- Does not handle product strategy or marketing.

### Tools & Permissions
- Access to codebase and CI/CD tools.
- Required: Node.js, React, email protocols (SMTP, IMAP).

### Communication
- Concise, direct, and technical.

### Collaboration & Escalation
- Reports to CTO.
- Escalates blockers to CTO.

## 2. DevOps Engineer

### Summary
Ensure reliable deployment, monitoring, and scaling of email infrastructure.

### Expertise & Responsibilities
- Manage CI/CD pipelines.
- Monitor system health and performance.
- Automate deployment and scaling processes.

### Priorities
1. Infrastructure reliability.
2. Automation of deployments.
3. Monitoring and alerts.

### Boundaries
- Does not develop application features.

### Tools & Permissions
- Access to cloud infrastructure and monitoring tools.
- Required: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform.

### Communication
- Clear, proactive, and data-driven.

### Collaboration & Escalation
- Reports to CTO.
- Escalates infrastructure issues to CTO.

## 3. QA Engineer

### Summary
Ensure email system reliability through rigorous testing.

### Expertise & Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans.
- Automate testing processes.
- Report and track bugs.

### Priorities
1. Test coverage.
2. Bug detection and reporting.
3. Automation of regression tests.

### Boundaries
- Does not develop features or infrastructure.

### Tools & Permissions
- Access to testing environments and bug tracking tools.
- Required: Selenium, Jest, Postman.

### Communication
- Detailed and precise.

### Collaboration & Escalation
- Reports to CTO.
- Escalates critical bugs to CTO.
